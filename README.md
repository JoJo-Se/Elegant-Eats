# Elegant Eats

Elegant Eats is a restaurant e-commerce website designed to make online food ordering simple, fast, and convenient. Customers can browse the menu, explore available dishes, add items to their cart, and place orders through a clean and responsive interface.

---

## About the Project

Elegant Eats was developed to provide a seamless online food ordering experience. The platform eliminates the need for customers to visit a physical restaurant by allowing them to explore the menu and order their favorite meals from anywhere.

---

## Features

* Browse a variety of food and beverage options
* View detailed menu items
* Add and remove items from the shopping cart
* Responsive design for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices
* Simple and intuitive user interface
* Smooth navigation and interactive user experience

---

## Purpose

The goal of Elegant Eats is to improve the food ordering experience by providing customers with a fast, reliable, and accessible online platform. It focuses on convenience while demonstrating modern front-end web development practices.

---

## Benefits

* Convenient online food ordering from any device
* Faster and more efficient ordering process
* Responsive design for an optimal user experience
* Clean and modern interface that is easy to navigate

---

## Tech Stack
